Understanding Carbonaceous Aerosols

Welcome to the Global Brown Carbon (BrC) Research and Visualization Portal. This platform integrates multi-wavelength optical properties, global spatial distributions, source apportionment profiles, and advanced absorption calculations to help decipher the climate impacts of complex atmospheric particles.

Black Carbon (BC)

A highly absorbing, potent short-lived climate forcer emitted from incomplete combustion of fossil fuels and biomass. BC strongly absorbs solar radiation across the entire UV-visible to near-infrared spectrum.

Organic Carbon (OC)

A broad class of carbon-based compounds emitted directly from combustion or formed secondarily in the atmosphere, traditionally considered purely scattering.

Brown Carbon (BrC)

The light-absorbing fraction of Organic Carbon. BrC exhibits strong wavelength-dependent absorption, absorbing heavily in the near-UV and visible ranges.
