Understanding Carbonaceous Aerosols

Welcome to the Global Brown Carbon (BrC) Research and Visualization Portal. This platform integrates multi-wavelength optical properties, global spatial distributions, source apportionment profiles, and advanced absorption calculations to help decipher the climate impacts of complex atmospheric particles.

Black Carbon (BC)

A highly absorbing, potent short-lived climate forcer emitted from incomplete combustion of fossil fuels and biomass. BC strongly absorbs solar radiation across the entire UV-visible to near-infrared spectrum, causing significant atmospheric warming.

Organic Carbon (OC)

A broad class of carbon-based compounds emitted directly from combustion or formed secondarily in the atmosphere. Traditionally, total OC has been considered a purely scattering aerosol, meaning it reflects sunlight and generally creates a net cooling effect on the climate.

Brown Carbon (BrC)

The light-absorbing fraction of Organic Carbon. Unlike BC, BrC exhibits strong wavelength-dependent absorption—it absorbs radiation heavily in the near-UV and visible ranges, but absorption drops to nearly zero in the near-infrared.

Why is this important?

Accurately distinguishing between these carbon fractions is critical for climate modeling. Because Brown Carbon absorbs incoming solar radiation, it partially offsets the cooling effect of scattering Organic Carbon. High concentrations of BrC from biomass burning (like agricultural fires and residential heating) can cause significant regional warming and accelerate snowmelt when deposited on glaciers. Understanding BrC's unique optical signature is essential to discerning its true climate impact.

BrC Absorption Calculation

Calculate $b_{\text{abs,BrC},\lambda}$ directly using component subtraction:

Calculated BrC Absorption ($b_{\text{abs,BrC},\lambda}$): 0.00 Mm⁻¹
$$b_{\text{abs,BrC},\lambda} = b_{\text{abs,Total},\lambda} - b_{\text{abs,BC,880}} \times \left(\frac{\lambda}{880}\right)^{-\text{AAE}_{\text{BC}}} - b_{\text{abs,Other},\lambda}$$
Symbol Definitions & Nomenclature:
  • $\lambda$: Wavelength of incident light ($\text{nm}$)
  • $\text{AAE}_{\text{BC}}$: Absorption Ångström Exponent for Black Carbon
  • $\text{AAE}_{\text{BrC}}$: Absorption Ångström Exponent for Brown Carbon
  • $b_{\text{abs,BC,880}}$: Measured or fixed Black Carbon light absorption coefficient at $880\text{ nm}$ ($\text{Mm}^{-1}$)
  • $b_{\text{abs,Total},\lambda}$: Total measured aerosol light absorption at wavelength $\lambda$ ($\text{Mm}^{-1}$)
  • $b_{\text{abs,BrC},\lambda}$: Brown Carbon light absorption coefficient at wavelength $\lambda$ ($\text{Mm}^{-1}$)
  • $b_{\text{abs,Other},\lambda}$: Light absorption by other aerosol components (e.g., mineral dust), assumed negligible ($\approx 0$) in this formulation.
